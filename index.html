What this notebook models

All qubits start at |0⟩. H, X, Y, Z, S, T, RX, RY, RZ, CX, CZ, and SWAP evolve a pure complex statevector. Every gate has a saved frame. A partial trace gives each qubit's reduced density matrix, Bloch coordinates, and purity.

The shot experiment samples the final computational-basis distribution using a reproducible xorshift32 generator. It does not collapse the displayed state, add measurement gates, model noise, or run on quantum hardware. Shot counts are simulated observations, not new amplitude calculations.

Limits: 1–6 qubits, 100 gates, 1–100,000 shots, rotation angles within ±8π, and circuit imports up to 100 KB. OpenQASM 2 export contains the unitary circuit only, with no classical register or measurements; shots and seed are saved in JSON. Save before refreshing.
